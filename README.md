</p>About me
I'm Raunak, a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, a hackathon builder, and a developer who enjoys working across the stack.

My projects range from a programming language and its tooling to PostgreSQL plan checks, collaborative coding, and satellite-based environmental monitoring. I like having something concrete to show: a working CLI, a usable app, or a demo that makes the idea clear.
- Building with: Python, TypeScript, Go, Kotlin, Dart, and SQL.
- Exploring: database internals, real-time collaboration, on-device AI, and offline-first architecture.
- Always improving: system design, testing, and the experience of the people using what I build.
